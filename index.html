Uta Note

Learn Japanese from the songs you love.

Paste the lyrics of a song you already know, and Uta Note turns it into a lesson you can follow line by line:

  • readings in kana or furigana, romaji and an English translation for every line;
  • a "Why?" explanation of how each line and word works, from grammar to the liberties lyrics take;
  • mark the lines you can say, and review the words you save as flashcards.

Your lyrics stay private to your account. The analyses and explanations are AI-generated, so treat them as a study aid.
